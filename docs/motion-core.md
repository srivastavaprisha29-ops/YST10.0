# MotionCore
Frame-indexed action tracks separate body, face, mouth, gaze, camera and props. Same-track overlaps are rejected; separate tracks can run concurrently. Externally aligned visemes can be converted to frame intervals. The layered compositor animates prepared RGBA artwork with position, rotation and opacity curves. It preserves source artwork rather than redrawing it, but rotation resampling may change pixel edges. It does not synthesize new poses, segment images, infer phonemes, generate speech or render a full video yet.

Reference-conditioned generation must preserve approved style and asset identity. For text-only projects establish an approved style bible before rendering. Next: pose-conditioned keyframes, action-state continuity, audio alignment, contact constraints, articulated rigs and actual video integration. Evaluate action completion, event timing, style fidelity and identity across shots. Public safety and rights checks remain mandatory. No live inference or test results claimed.
